What design principle did you find most useful?

I found repetition to be the most useful design principle because having a consistent 
theme across slides creates visual unity and cohesion. By reusing the same colors, fonts, 
and layout elements, I was able to focus more on the content instead of worrying about 
designing each slide from scratch, which also made the presentation look more organized 
and professional.

What part of Canva was easiest/hardest to use?

The easiest part of using Canva for me was its drag-and-drop interface, as it made 
arranging elements quick and intuitive. However, the most challenging or time-consuming 
part was deciding how to modify templates to fit my own needs while still maintaining 
consistency and clarity in the overall design.

What did you learn about visual communication?

I learned that properly organizing information plays a major role in effective visual 
communication. When content is structured clearly and related elements are grouped well, 
the presentation becomes easier to understand and allows the audience to grasp key points 
more efficiently.
