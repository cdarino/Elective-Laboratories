Which design was easiest?

The social media post was the easiest to design due to its smaller size and simpler 
layout compared to the other formats.

What branding elements were applied?

Consistent branding elements, such as colors, fonts, and logo placement, were applied 
across all three designs to maintain a cohesive visual identity.

How you ensured readability and hierarchy.

Readability and hierarchy were maintained by positioning headings at the top, 
placing the main message in the center, and highlighting the call-to-action clearly.

What you learned about creating social media graphics.

I learned the importance of applying consistent branding, which ensures a professional 
appearance and allows for quicker, high-quality graphic creation in the future.
