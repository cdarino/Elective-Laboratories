What inspired your branding?

My branding was inspired by my own personality, which I wanted the design to reflect 
clearly. I chose a clean and minimal style to represent how I approach my work, making 
the branding feel personal while still professional.

How did you choose your palette?

I chose a dark blue color palette because it best reflects my personality and the image 
I wanted to convey. Dark blue communicates calmness, focus, and reliability, which aligned 
well with how I wanted my brand to be perceived.

What was challenging in creating the logo?

The most challenging part of creating the logo was translating my personality into a 
visual form. Since the design was meant to represent me personally, I felt pressure to 
ensure that it accurately reflected who I am, which made the design process more 
deliberate and time-consuming.

How do you plan to use this branding in future projects?

Because this branding closely matches my personality, I plan to continue using it in 
future projects. Maintaining the same visual identity will help create consistency across 
my work and make my designs more recognizable over time.
